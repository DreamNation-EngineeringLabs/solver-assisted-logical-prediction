# ICLR 2027 official style files

Downloaded 12 Sept 2026 from the author guidelines page:

    https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines
    https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip

Kept here as the source of truth; `paper/final/` gets a copy so that directory
stays flat and self-contained (it must compile in a sandboxed LaTeX editor).
Only `iclr2027_conference.sty` and `.bst` are needed — `fancyhdr.sty` and
`natbib.sty` also ship in the zip but tectonic resolves both from CTAN, and
`math_commands.tex` is optional notation this paper does not use.

Do not edit these files. The layout they impose is the venue's.
